All batteries are ideal and identical, and so are all bulbs. Three batteries are connected in series, but the top one is the wrong way round. They drive bulb A in parallel with the bulbs B and C in series (left). The reference circuit is one bulb $L_0$ connected to one battery (right).

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What voltage do the three batteries give together, in units of the voltage $\Delta V_0$ of one battery?
\abc Is each of the bulbs A, B and C brighter than $L_0$, equally bright, or less bright? Rank all four bulbs.
\abc A student says: ``There are three batteries, so A is three times as bright as $L_0$.'' What is wrong with this?
\end{abcliste}
