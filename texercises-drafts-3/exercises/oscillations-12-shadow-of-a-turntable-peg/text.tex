A peg stands upright on a record player's turntable, \rpO\ from the axis. The turntable turns at \nO\ (45 revolutions per minute). A lamp far away to the side throws the shadow of the peg onto a wall facing the lamp; the shadow moves back and forth along the wall. Next to it, a body of mass \mO\ hangs on a spring and oscillates horizontally on an air track, exactly in step with the shadow.
\begin{abcliste}
\abc What are the amplitude and the period of the shadow's motion?
\abc What is its angular frequency?
\abc What spring constant must the spring have for the body to stay in step with the shadow?
\abc How fast is the shadow at most, and where is the peg at that moment?
\end{abcliste}
