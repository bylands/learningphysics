A capacitor of \CpO\ is charged to \UzO. At $t = 0$ it is connected to a coil of inductance \LiO\ (resistance negligible). The charge $Q$ on the capacitor and the current $I = \dot Q$ then oscillate; the voltages across coil and capacitor add up to zero: $L\,\dot I + Q/C = 0$.
\begin{abcliste}
\abc Compare with a body on a spring, $m\,\ddot y = -D\,y$. Which quantities of the circuit play the roles of the displacement $y$, the velocity $v$, the mass $m$ and the spring constant $D$?
\abc Calculate the angular frequency and the period of the oscillation.
\abc When is the current largest for the first time, and how large is it then?
\end{abcliste}
