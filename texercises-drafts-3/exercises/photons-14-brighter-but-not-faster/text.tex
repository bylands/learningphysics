A photocell with a potassium cathode (work function \WaO) is lit with blue light of wavelength \laO. At a light power of \PlO\ on the cathode, the photocurrent stops at a counter-voltage of $U_0 = \UsO$; without counter-voltage, the saturation current is \IsO.

The light power is now raised by the factor $k = \kfO$.
\begin{abcliste}
\abc What does the wave model predict for the stopping voltage and for the saturation current?
\abc The measurement gives the same stopping voltage, \UsO, and a saturation current of \IbP{-6}{3}. Which prediction holds, which fails?
\abc Explain both observations with photons. Calculate the photon energy, check the stopping voltage with it, and compare the number of photons per second with the number of electrons per second, before and after.
\end{abcliste}
