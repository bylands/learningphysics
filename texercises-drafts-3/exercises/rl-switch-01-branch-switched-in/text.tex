A battery with $V = \VbO$ feeds a resistor $R_1 = \RaO$ and then two parallel branches: a resistor $R_2 = \RbO$ in series with an inductor $L = \LcO$, and a resistor $R_3 = \RcO$ behind a switch S. The battery and the inductor have no resistance of their own. The switch has been open for a long time; at $t = 0$ it is closed. The arrows show the positive direction of the currents.

\begin{center}
\begin{circuitikz}[scale=0.9, transform shape]
 \draw (0,0) to[battery1, invert, l=$V$] (0,4) to[R, l=$R_1$, i>^=$I_1$] (3,4) -- (6.5,4);
 \node at (0.4,2.25) {$+$}; \node at (0.4,1.75) {$-$};
 \draw (4,4) to[R, l_=$R_2$, i>^=$I_2$] (4,2) to[L, l_=$L$] (4,0);
 \draw (6.5,4) to[R, l=$R_3$, i>_=$I_3$] (6.5,2) to[nos, l=S] (6.5,0) -- (0,0);
 \fill (4,4) circle (2pt) (4,0) circle (2pt);
\end{circuitikz}
\end{center}

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What current flows through the inductor before $t = 0$?
\abc Find the currents $I_1$, $I_2$ and $I_3$ and the voltage $V_L$ across the inductor (in the direction of its arrow) immediately after S is closed. How large is the emf induced in the inductor?
\abc At what rate does the inductor current change at this moment? Does it grow or fall?
\end{abcliste}
