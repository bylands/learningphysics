A pointer of length \AO\ turns anticlockwise about its fixed end at a frequency of \fO, two turns per second. At $t = 0$ it points to the right. A small body is always at the same height as the tip of the pointer: it moves up and down along the vertical axis, $y$ being its height above the centre.
