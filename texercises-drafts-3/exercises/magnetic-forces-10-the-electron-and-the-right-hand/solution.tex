\abc \result{\text{Step 1}} is wrong. The right hand gives the force on a positive charge. The force $\vec F = q\,\vec v\times\vec B$ changes sign with the charge, so a negative charge such as the electron needs the left hand (or the right hand, and then the force reversed). Steps 2 to 4 are done right: with the right hand, thumb to the right and index finger into the page, the middle finger does point up.

\abc With the left hand: thumb to the right (along $\vec v$), index finger into the page (along $\vec B$), the middle finger points \result{\text{down the page}}. Check: $\vec v\times\vec B$ points up, and the factor $q = -e$ turns it round.
\end{abcliste}
