\begin{abcliste}
\abc The field lines (radial, pointing outwards) and the circles are right, but \result{\text{the spacing of the circles}} is wrong. For equal steps of potential, the equipotentials are closer together where the field is stronger. The field of a point charge gets weaker outwards, $E = k\,\frac{Q}{r^2}$, so the circles must get further and further apart outwards.

\abc Round a point charge $V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\,r}$, so $r = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\,V}$:
\begin{align}
 r_1 &= \raF = \frac{\Qc}{4\pi\cdot\nceps\cdot\Va} = \ra \approx \result{\raP{-2}{3}} \\
 r_2 &= \rbF = \frac{\Qc}{4\pi\cdot\nceps\cdot\Vb} = \rb \approx \result{\rbP{-2}{3}} \\
 r_3 &= \rcF = \frac{\Qc}{4\pi\cdot\nceps\cdot\Vc} = \rc \approx \result{\rcP{-2}{3}} \\
 r_4 &= \rdF = \frac{\Qc}{4\pi\cdot\nceps\cdot\Vd} = \rd \approx \result{\rdP{-2}{3}}
\end{align}
Only the inner circle is right. The gaps grow from about \SI{0.8}{\cm} to \SI{1.6}{\cm} and \SI{4.8}{\cm}.
