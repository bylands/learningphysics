Three identical bulbs A, B and C are connected in series to an ideal battery.

\begin{center}
\begin{circuitikz}[scale=0.9, transform shape]

 \node at (-0.4,1.75) {$+$}; \node at (-0.4,1.25) {$-$};
 \foreach \x/\p in {1/P,3/Q,5/R,7/S} {\fill (\x,3) circle (2pt); \node[below] at (\x,2.9) {\p};}
\end{circuitikz}
\end{center}

\begin{abcliste}
\abc A wire is connected from Q to R, across bulb B. How does the brightness of each bulb change compared with the circuit without the wire: brighter, the same, dimmer, or off?
\abc This wire is removed, and a wire from P to R is connected instead, across A and B. How bright is each bulb now, compared with the circuit without a wire and with (a)?
\end{abcliste}
