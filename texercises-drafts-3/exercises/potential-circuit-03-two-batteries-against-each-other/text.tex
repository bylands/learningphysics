A \SI{12}{\V} battery and a \SI{4}{\V} battery are connected against each other in a loop with two lamps $L_1$ and $L_2$. The current $I$ flows as shown. The voltage across $L_1$ is \SI{5}{\V}, across $L_2$ \SI{3}{\V}. The potential is measured from the negative terminal of the \SI{12}{\V} battery at A: $\varphi_A = \SI{0}{\V}$.
