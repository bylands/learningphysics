A \SI{9}{\V} battery drives a current $I$ through two lamps $L_1$ and $L_2$. The voltage across $L_1$ is \SI{6}{\V}, across $L_2$ \SI{3}{\V}. The potential is measured from the negative terminal of the battery at A: $\varphi_A = \SI{0}{\V}$.
