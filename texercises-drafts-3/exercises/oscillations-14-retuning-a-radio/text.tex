The tuning circuit of a radio is an LC circuit with a coil of \LiO\ and a variable capacitor. It is tuned to a station at \faO; to receive another station at \fbO, the circuit must oscillate at that frequency.
\begin{abcliste}
\abc What is the capacitance now?
\abc By what factor must the capacitance change? What is it then?
\abc A classmate says: ``The frequency has to go down by the factor $f_1/f_2 = 1.66$, so I make the capacitance smaller by that factor.'' Judge the answer using the comparison with a body on a spring.
\end{abcliste}
